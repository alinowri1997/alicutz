## Why maintenance matters during business travel

A sharp fade can lose definition quickly when schedules are tight.

A short maintenance visit protects your presentation quality without forcing a full style reset.

## Priority zones for fast refinement

1. Side profile transition around temples.
2. Neckline contour and lower fade blend.
3. Beard line balance against jaw width.

These three zones create most of the visual impact in professional settings.

## Product strategy for hotel stays

Use lightweight matte texture in daytime.

For evening client dinners, increase definition slightly without adding heavy shine.

## Timing strategy

Book your maintenance session at least one day before key meetings.

This gives your haircut time to settle naturally while preserving a clean, premium look.
