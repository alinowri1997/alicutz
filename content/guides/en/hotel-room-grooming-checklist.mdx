## The 3-layer grooming framework

Use this sequence: hygiene, structure, finish.

It keeps your routine fast without sacrificing detail.

## Layer 1: Hygiene foundation

- Quick face cleanse.
- Light moisturizer.
- Hair refreshed with minimal water and towel dry.

## Layer 2: Structural details

1. Temple and sideburn symmetry.
2. Beard neckline and cheek line alignment.
3. Ear contour and nape cleanup.

These lines control how polished you look from every angle.

## Layer 3: Professional finish

Use low-shine styling product for daylight meetings.

For evening events, a little more texture can add depth without looking heavy.

## Quick pre-meeting audit

- Is your profile line clean?
- Is your beard balanced with jaw width?
- Is your hair volume controlled at the crown?

If yes, your grooming is ready.
