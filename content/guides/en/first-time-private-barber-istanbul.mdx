## Start with clarity, not complexity

Your first private barber session should feel simple.

Clear goals produce better outcomes than trend-heavy requests.

## What to send before booking confirmation

1. One front-view reference.
2. One side-profile reference.
3. Your preferred level of beard definition.
4. Your available time window.

This reduces guesswork and protects consistency.

## During the first consultation

Ask for a structure that matches your real schedule.

If you style your hair in under five minutes, say it clearly. Technique and finish should match your daily behavior.

## Aftercare for first-time clients

- Avoid drastic product changes in the first week.
- Keep side profile clean with light grooming.
- Rebook before shape loss becomes visible.

Consistency is the foundation of premium grooming.
