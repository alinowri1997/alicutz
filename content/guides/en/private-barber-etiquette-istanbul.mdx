## Why etiquette matters for private appointments

A private barber appointment is not only about the cut. It is about efficiency, discretion, and comfort in your own environment.

When the setup is correct, you get better precision and a calmer result.

## Before your barber arrives

1. Share your preferred haircut style through WhatsApp.
2. Mention your time window clearly.
3. Prepare a clean chair near bright natural or warm overhead light.
4. Keep your schedule open for at least one hour.

## During the consultation

Start with your priority: sharp fade, balanced beard, or an easy-to-maintain business profile.

Your barber can adjust details based on growth pattern, crown direction, and density.

## Post-cut routine for 72 hours

- Avoid aggressive heat styling on day one.
- Keep neckline clean with a soft rinse.
- Use light matte product rather than heavy pomade.

## Final standard

Premium grooming means consistent confidence, not drastic experiments.

A private appointment works best when communication is clear and expectations are realistic.
